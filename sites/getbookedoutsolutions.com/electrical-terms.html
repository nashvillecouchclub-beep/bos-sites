What we guarantee

Every piece of work in your package gets done every month, and shows in your Friday report. These have exact clocks:

  • New quotes you forward are contacted within 48 hours.
  • Your old 90-day pile of quotes is contacted within 5 business days (customers with an email address).
  • Fixes to your Google listing are submitted by the 3rd business day. Submitting is our part; Google decides when they show.
  • Every email inquiry is answered the same day.
  • If we run ads for you: worked every business day, with a written note every week on what changed and why.

We guarantee our part of every piece: the writing, the sending, the posting, the submitting. Not the printer, the mail, Google or your customers. We never promise jobs, revenue or a spot on Google.

If your area is locked to you

We never work for or email another shop in your trade in your zone. If we break that, your lock is free for the rest of the year.

How to cancel

  1. Reply "cancel" to any email from us.
  2. You get a note back with the date your service ends.
  3. Nothing more is charged. Your site, your domain and your lists are yours to keep.

Changes to what we write for you

Two rounds of changes on anything we write for you. After that we work it out together by email.

Ad money

If we run ads for you, the ad budget is your own, paid by you to Google, on top of our price. Our price does not go up when your ad budget does.
