How to give us access to your Facebook Page

This lets Booked Out post, answer comments, see results and run approved ads for your Facebook Page. You stay the owner, we never see your password, and you can remove us any time. About 2 minutes, on a computer.

Have these ready

  • A computer (Meta's business settings work best there)
  • Admin access to your Facebook Page's business portfolio in Meta Business Suite

Step by step

  1. Send us your business portfolio IDGo to business.facebook.com, open Settings, then Business info. Under your business name you'll see Business portfolio ID followed by a long number. Copy it and send it to us by replying to our email. We use it to send you a partner request.
  2. Open PartnersWhen we tell you the request is sent, go back to Settings and click Partners in the left menu (not Requests). Booked Out Solutions shows with a Request received tag. Click Review and approve.
  3. Get started and NextClick Get started on Meta's intro. The next screen may say Booked Out Solutions is Unverified. That's normal while Meta reviews our business. Click Next.
  4. Pick your PageClick the Select Facebook Pages box, tick your business Page, then click Done.
  5. Turn on four permissionsTurn on Content, Community activity, Ads and Insights. Leave Messages and calls, Revenue and the rest off. Click Review selection.
  6. ApproveCheck it lists your Page with those four permissions, then click Approve request. You'll see Booked Out Solutions was assigned to 1 asset.

If something doesn't look right

  • Don't see the request under Requests? That's expected. Partner requests show under Settings, then Partners.
  • No Business info or Partners in Settings? Your Page may not be in a business portfolio yet. Tell us and we'll walk you through making one (free, about 2 minutes).
  • The roles on the review screen look swapped (your business called Media agency)? That's only Meta's label. It doesn't change the access you give.
  • Want to remove us later? Settings, Partners, click Booked Out Solutions, then remove access.

What happens next

We start on your Page right away. Ads never spend until you approve the plan and budget.
