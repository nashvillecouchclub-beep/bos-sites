How to put your card on file and sign the agreement

Payments go through Square's secure checkout. Booked Out never sees or stores your full card number. About 3 minutes.

Have these ready

  • Your debit or credit card
  • Your agreement (we email it with your checkout link)

Step by step

  1. Read the one-page agreementIt says what's in your package, the price, when the first charge happens, and how to cancel. If anything looks off, email us before you pay.
  2. Open secure checkoutTap Open secure checkout on your setup page. It opens Square, the payment company.
  3. Enter your cardType your card number, expiration date, security code and ZIP code into Square's form.
  4. ConfirmTap the button to confirm. Square emails you a receipt or confirmation.

If something doesn't look right

  • My card was declined: check the ZIP code matches your card's billing address, or try another card.
  • I need an invoice for my bookkeeper: email us and we'll send one.

What happens next

Your setup page marks this step done, and your work starts on the date in your agreement.
