How to verify your Google Business Profile with a video

Google asks the owner to prove the business is real. Most of the time it asks for a short phone video. Google's rules are strict, so plan it before you press record. About 5 minutes, plus a short walk.

Have these ready

  • Your phone (it has to be recorded on a phone, inside the Business Profile)
  • Plan your route: where you'll start, what you'll show
  • Something only the business can access: a key to the office or a branded van, a work tablet, a business invoice or utility bill with the business name

Step by step

  1. Plan what to showGoogle wants three things in one video: where you are (street signs, building number, nearby businesses), that the business exists (your sign, or for service businesses your tools, equipment, branded shirts or truck), and that you run it (unlock the door or van, open the work area, or show a business bill with your business name).
  2. Start from your Business ProfileOn your phone, open your Business Profile and tap Get verified. Enter your phone number if asked. Tap Business video, then Next. Allow camera, microphone and location if asked.
  3. Record one continuous videoTap Start recording. Keep it to one take, at least 30 seconds, with no cuts. Start outside with the street or building, show your sign or equipment, then show you unlocking or using something only the business can.
  4. Don't show private thingsDon't show bank, tax or ID numbers, private information, or other people's faces.
  5. Review and uploadTap stop, watch it back, tap Try again if something's missing, then tap Upload video.
  6. Wait for GoogleGoogle reviews it within about 5 business days. If it's rejected, Google says why: tap Review issues and record a new video with everything from the first one plus what was missing.

If something doesn't look right

  • It won't let me upload a video I already recorded: that's Google's rule. It has to be recorded live inside the Business Profile.
  • I work from home or from my truck: you're a service-area business. Show the street, your tools or branded truck, and unlocking the truck or a business bill with your business name.
  • Not sure your sign or office is ready? Wait. A rejected video only adds time. Tell us and we'll help you plan it.
