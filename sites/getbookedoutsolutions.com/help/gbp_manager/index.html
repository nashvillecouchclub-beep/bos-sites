How to add Booked Out to your Google Business Profile

This lets us post updates, keep your hours and services right, and help with reviews on your Google profile. You stay the owner and can remove us any time. About 2 minutes.

Have these ready

  • Sign in with the Google account that OWNS your Business Profile (only an owner can add people)
  • Our address to add: hello@getbookedoutsolutions.com

Step by step

  1. Open your Business ProfileSigned in to the Google account that owns your profile, search Google for: my business. Your profile panel shows at the top of the results. (You can also go to business.google.com and click your business name.) On a phone, open the Google Maps app, tap your picture, then Your Business Profile.
  2. Open Business Profile settingsClick the three dots at the top right of your business panel, then Business Profile settings.
  3. Open People and accessTap People and access. You'll see who manages the profile now.
  4. Tap AddTap Add at the top left.
  5. Enter our email and choose ManagerType hello@getbookedoutsolutions.com. Under Access, choose Manager (not Owner). Tap Invite.
  6. DoneGoogle emails us the invitation and we accept it, usually the same day. Google can take up to 7 days to give a new manager every feature, which is normal.

If something doesn't look right

  • I don't see People and access: you're probably signed in as a manager, not an owner. Sign in with the owner account, or ask whoever set up the profile.
  • Which account owns my profile? Google sends profile emails to the owner account. Search your inbox for 'Business Profile'.
  • To remove us later: same place, People and access, tap our name, then Remove.

What happens next

Once we're in, we fix anything missing on your profile and start your weekly posts.
