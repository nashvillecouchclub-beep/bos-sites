How to send us job photos

Real photos of your work, trucks and team do better than stock photos, every time. About 5 minutes.

Have these ready

  • Your phone's photo gallery

Step by step

  1. Pick 5 to 10 photosYour trucks, your team in uniform, your shop, and finished work (a clean install, a new panel, a repaired line). Before and after pairs are great.
  2. Keep them private-safeSkip customers' faces, house numbers, street signs at a customer's home, and family photos inside homes.
  3. Send themReply to our welcome email and attach the photos. On iPhone tap the photo icon; on Android tap the paperclip.

If something doesn't look right

  • Email says the photos are too big: send them in two or three emails.

What happens next

We use them on your Google profile, posts and website, with your logo where it fits.
