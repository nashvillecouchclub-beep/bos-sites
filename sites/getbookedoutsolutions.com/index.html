Straight talk

What we promise, and what we don't.

What we promise: the work

In a paid month, every piece of work in your package gets done, and you see each one in your Friday report. Some have exact clocks:

  • New quotes you forward get their first note within 48 hours.
  • Your old 90-day pile of quotes is contacted within 5 business days.
  • Every email inquiry is answered the same day.
  • Fixes to your Google listing are submitted by the 3rd business day.
  • Postcards, posts, review replies, website pages and everything else in your package go out on schedule.
  • If we run your ads: worked every business day, with a written note every week.

If we miss any piece of your package, your next month is free.

What we don't promise: jobs

Nobody honest can. We can't control Google, the mail or your customers, so we don't put our name on them.

We promise what is fully in our hands, and every Friday you see exactly what was done.
