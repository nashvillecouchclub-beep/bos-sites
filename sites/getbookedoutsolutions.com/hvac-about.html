Rickey Hallor

I owned four dry cleaners for years. One of them was voted best in town three years running.

Ask me why and the answer is short: we answered everyone.

Every question, every complaint, every customer who hadn't been in for a while. We also made pickup easy and ran the shop like a well-tuned machine, but the answering is what people noticed.

What I learned is that customers pick the business that follows up. Not the cheapest one, and not always the best one. The one that wrote back.

I started Booked Out Solutions in Smyrna, Tennessee to do that follow-up for trade shops, where the owner is usually on a job when the customer writes.
