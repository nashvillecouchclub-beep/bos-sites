Questions owners ask

Short answers.

What's the catch?

There isn't a hidden one. The look is free and yours to keep. The first 30 days are free. You sign up with a card on Square’s secure checkout, and nothing is charged until day 30. Reply "cancel" before then and you pay nothing. Day 30 is the first point where it costs money.

What does it cost?

The free look and your first 30 days cost nothing. After that it's one flat price every 30 days for the package you pick, with no long-term contract. Your price page comes by email with your results. Want it sooner? Ask and we'll send it.

What if nothing comes back in the first 30 days?

Reply "cancel" before day 30 and you pay nothing, and we stop.

How fast does it start?

Your free look arrives within 2 business days. In your free 30 days, the first notes go out as soon as you've OK'd how they read. In a paid month, new quotes get their first note within 48 hours and your old 90-day pile is contacted within 5 business days.

Do my techs have to do anything?

No. Nothing to install, no script, no asking for reviews at the door. We ask your customers for reviews by email. Your part is about 20 minutes, one time: forward your old quotes or a photo of the pile, send a list of recent customers, add us to your Google profile, and OK the first notes.

Do you write or buy reviews?

Never. We ask your real customers, every one of them, with the same link. We don't write reviews, buy them, or ask only the happy ones.

Is it only old quotes?

No. Old quotes are where most shops see it first, but it's the whole growth side of the shop: every inquiry answered the same day, every review replied to, your Google profile fixed and posted on every week, past customers reached, website fixes, postcards mailed, ads run for you on the higher packages, and a report every Friday on what we did and what it brought in.

Does the free look cost anything?

No. It's free, there's nothing to pay, and it's yours to keep whether you ever hire us or not.

Is there a contract?

No long-term contract. Cancel anytime by replying "cancel" to any email. Your service runs to the end of the month you paid for, then billing stops.

Will I have to get on the phone?

No. We don't make sales calls, and the work we do for you happens by email, at whatever hour suits you. If you'd rather talk, you can call Booker, our AI assistant, any time, day or night, and ask him anything about Booked Out.

How much of my time does it take?

About 20 minutes, one time, at the start. After that, a reply when you want to. Nothing goes out in your name until you've OK'd how it reads.

Do you guarantee more jobs?

No, and be careful with anyone who does. We guarantee the work: every piece of your package, done every month, with dates on the big ones. If we miss any piece, your next month is free.

Is this AI?

Yes, and we'll always tell you so. AI does the writing and the work, a second AI model checks every send, Rickey reviews samples himself, and nothing new goes out in your name until you OK the first one. No sales calls and no texts. If you have the AI receptionist, it always says it's an AI receptionist.

I already have a marketing company.

You do. Your Google profile, reviews and review replies stay yours, always. You add us as a manager on the profile and can take us off any day. If you leave, your site, your domain and your lists are yours to keep.

Reply "cancel" to any email. You get a note back with the date your service ends. Nothing more is charged after that.
