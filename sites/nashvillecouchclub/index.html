Questions

Good to know

Are the pieces cleaned?

Every piece is checked when it comes in, and anything that needs it is cleaned before it's listed. Each listing says what was done to that piece.

Can I see a piece before I buy?

Yes. Text us and we'll send more photos or a video, or set a time for you to see it in person.

How do I hold a piece?

Text (615) 956-2113 with the piece you want. We'll confirm it's still available and set a delivery or pickup time.

How do I pay?

At delivery or pickup, with cash, card, Zelle, Cash App or Venmo.

How far do you deliver?

About 25 miles from Smyrna, which covers most of the Nashville area. Farther out? Text us and ask.

Do you take my old couch?

Yes. When you buy from us, we haul your old couch away for free.
