What a first couples session looks like

Calling a couples therapist can feel like a big step. A lot of couples put it off because they don't know what will happen once they sit down. So here is what the start of couples work looks like with me.

Call (615) 200-0063Free 15-minute consult

Before the first session

It starts with a free 15-minute phone call. You tell me a little about what's going on, and I tell you how I work. You can ask anything. If it seems like a good fit, we set up the first session. If it doesn't, I'll try to point you to someone who is.

The first few sessions are about getting to know you

Couples work with me starts with 2 to 3 assessment sessions. We meet together, and I also meet with each of you on your own. I ask about how you met, what has been good, what has been hard, and what each of you hopes will change. I also ask about past relationships and general health history. Nothing is off limits, and nothing has to be said before you're ready.

These sessions are not about picking a side. My job is to understand both of you and the pattern you get stuck in together.

What it feels like in the room

Most people are nervous at first. That's normal. Some couples come in calm. Some come in mid-fight. Both are okay. I'll slow things down so each of you gets heard. I'll stop us if things heat up too much. You won't be asked to air everything on day one.

Then we make a plan

After the assessment, I share what I'm seeing and we build a treatment plan together. We set goals in your words. Then we start the work. I've completed Level 1 training in Gottman Method Couples Therapy, and I draw on it for tools around conflict, trust and connection. We look at the goals again as we go, so you always know where things stand.

A few practical things

  • Couples intake is $195, and couples sessions after that are $175.
  • Sessions are in person on Tuesdays at my Murfreesboro office, or by video on Wednesdays for couples in Tennessee.
  • I'm not in-network with insurance plans yet, so sessions are private pay. I can give you a superbill.

What if one of you isn't sure?

That's common. Often one partner is ready and the other has doubts. A first session is not a promise to keep coming. It's a chance to see what it's like. You can read more about that in my article on when one partner is unsure.

If you're thinking about it, the free consult is a low-pressure place to start.
