How video sessions work

  • You need to be physically in Tennessee during each session. That's a licensing rule.
  • Join from a private space where you can talk freely. I'll be in a private space too.
  • Cameras stay on for the whole session.
  • Never join while driving. A parked car is fine.
  • At the start of each session I'll confirm where you are, in case we need to reach help.
  • Video sessions are on Wednesdays, 9 a.m.-5 p.m. (Central).
  • You'll get a secure video link by email before each session.
  • EMDR sessions, EMDR intensives and couples intensives are in person only, at my Murfreesboro office. In-person sessions are on Tuesdays.

Couples can join from the same room or from two places. If you're joining together, sit close enough that I can see you both.
