How telehealth therapy works in Tennessee

Therapy by video can save a drive, fit around work, and make it easier to show up. If you're thinking about video sessions, here's how they work with me.

Call (615) 200-0063Free 15-minute consult

You need to be in Tennessee

I'm licensed in Tennessee, so you need to be physically in Tennessee during each video session. That's a licensing rule, not a preference. If you travel, we can plan around it. At the start of each session I'll confirm where you are, in case we ever need to reach help.

What you need

  • A private space where you can talk freely. I'll be in a private space too.
  • A phone, tablet or computer with a camera.
  • A steady internet connection.

You'll get a secure video link by email before each session. Cameras stay on for the whole session.

Never join while driving

Please don't join while you're driving. A parked car is fine if it's the most private place you have.

Couples can join together or apart

If you're joining together, sit close enough that I can see you both. If you're in two places, that works too.

When video sessions happen

Video sessions are on Wednesdays, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. Central. In-person sessions are on Tuesdays at my Murfreesboro office.

What isn't by video

EMDR sessions, EMDR intensives and couples intensives are in person only. We do that work together at the office.

Is video therapy as helpful?

For a lot of people, video works well for talk therapy. Some people prefer being in the room. We can talk about what fits you on the free consult call. You can also switch between video and in person if that works better.

Fees

  • Individual sessions are $150 (55-60 minutes). Couples sessions are $175.
  • Sessions are private pay, and I can give you a superbill for out-of-network benefits.

The free 15-minute consult is by phone. It's a good time to ask any questions about how video sessions would work for you.
