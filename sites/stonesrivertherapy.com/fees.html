Good Faith Estimate

Under the law, health care providers need to give people who don't have insurance, or who aren't using insurance, an estimate of the bill for services. You have the right to receive a Good Faith Estimate for the total expected cost of any non-emergency services. You can ask for one before you schedule, and you'll get one in writing before your first session. If you receive a bill that is at least $400 more than your Good Faith Estimate, you can dispute the bill. Read the full notice.
