Why this work matters to me

My Master of Social Work studies at the University of Southern California focused on trauma and the adjustments that come with military life. I work with couples and with adults working through trauma, so I see how the two connect.

Who I see

  • Veterans, 18 and up.
  • Spouses and partners of Veterans and service members.
  • Couples where one or both partners served.
  • National Guard and Reserve members and their partners.

What we can work on

  • Trauma, with EMDR or talk therapy, at a pace that feels safe to you.
  • Feeling on edge, numb, or far away from the people you love.
  • The same fight on repeat, or the strain after a deployment, a move or leaving the service.
  • Couples therapy using the Gottman Method, weekly or as a focused 3-hour intensive. Couples work and intensives aren't the right fit where there is ongoing abuse. If that's part of the picture, I'll help find the right kind of support.

Where, when and fees

  • In person on Tuesdays at 212 Heritage Park Drive, Murfreesboro.
  • By video on Wednesdays from anywhere in Tennessee.
  • EMDR and intensives are in person only.
  • Private pay, with superbills for out-of-network benefits. I'm not a VA Community Care or TRICARE provider at this time. See the Fees page.
