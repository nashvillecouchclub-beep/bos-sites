Rebuilding trust

After a breach of trust, where do you start?

Finding out about an affair can turn everything upside down, for both partners. Some couples want to rebuild. Some aren't sure yet. Either way, you deserve a calm place to sort it out.

Book a free 15-minute consult

A steady place to talk about what happened

I work with couples navigating a breach of trust, whether that's a physical affair, an emotional one, or secrets that came out. My job isn't to take sides or to tell you whether to stay together. It's to help you both understand what happened and decide what comes next, together.

I completed Gottman Institute training in Treating Affairs and Trauma, along with Level 1 training in Gottman Method Couples Therapy.

What this work usually includes

  • Making room for the hurt partner's questions and feelings
  • Understanding what was happening in the relationship before
  • Rebuilding small, everyday trust
  • Deciding together what the relationship looks like from here

Before we start

For couples work to be safe, the affair needs to have ended. If it hasn't, or if there is ongoing abuse or active addiction, I'll talk with you about other support first. That isn't a judgment. It's about what will actually help.

Not sure your partner will come? You can start with the free consult on your own.
