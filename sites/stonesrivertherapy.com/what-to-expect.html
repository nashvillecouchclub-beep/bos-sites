What to expect

Getting started, step by step.

Knowing what's coming can make the first step easier. Here's how it works from the first call to your first session.

Book a free 15-minute consult

  1. The free 15-minute consult

    A short phone call. You share a little about what's going on, ask any questions, and we decide together whether to book. I'll also confirm you're in Tennessee and go over fees, so there are no surprises.

  2. Forms and payment details

    After your consult call, I'll go over your forms and payment details with you before your first session. Please don't email or text insurance cards or health details.

  3. Your Good Faith Estimate

    If you're paying privately, you'll get a written Good Faith Estimate of your costs before your first session.

  4. Your first session

    Individual first sessions are about getting to know you and what you want to work on. For couples, the first 2 to 3 sessions are an assessment: how you met, your history together and apart, and your health history. Both partners come, and each also has a little time with me alone.

  5. A plan we agree on

    We set goals together and check them along the way.
