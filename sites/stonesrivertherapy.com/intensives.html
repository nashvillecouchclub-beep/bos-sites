More time in one sitting.

Some people would rather work in a focused 3-hour block than wait a week between sessions. An intensive is one option. Weekly sessions are always available too.

What an intensive is

An intensive is a longer session, about 3 hours, with breaks built in. Before we start, we set one clear goal together. We stay with that goal, and anything new that comes up gets saved for a follow-up so it isn't rushed.

Intensives are in person. Both couples and EMDR intensives happen at my office at 212 Heritage Park Drive in Murfreesboro. They aren't offered by video.

Couples intensive

3 hours, $525

In person at my Murfreesboro office. Each partner shares their top concerns ahead of time. We work on one at a time, with a clear structure for hard conversations.

EMDR intensive

3 hours, $450

In person at my Murfreesboro office. Many people start with a few 90-minute EMDR sessions first, then move to a longer block if it fits.

Who it fits

Intensives can fit people with busy schedules, couples who want focused time together, or someone who already knows what they want to work on. We talk first in the free consult to see if an intensive makes sense for you.

An intensive isn't the right fit during a crisis, while an affair is ongoing, with active addiction, or where there is ongoing abuse. In those cases, I'll help you find the right kind of support.

Written terms

  • You get the terms in writing before we schedule: what's included, the fee, and cancellation.
  • Cancellation for an intensive: please give at least 2 weeks' notice. Real emergencies are handled case by case.
  • You get a Good Faith Estimate before the intensive.
  • We check in after the intensive, and short follow-up sessions are available.
  • An intensive is not crisis care. If you're in crisis, call or text 988.
