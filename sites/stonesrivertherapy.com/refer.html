Who I see

  • Adults 18 and up.
  • Couples: the same fight on repeat, feeling like roommates, thinking about marriage or separating, and recovery after an affair.
  • Adults working through trauma, with EMDR or talk therapy.
  • Focused 3-hour intensives, in person, for couples and for EMDR.

Who I'm not the right fit for

  • Anyone under 18.
  • Anyone who won't be physically in Tennessee during sessions.
  • Crisis care. For a crisis, please point people to 988 or 911.
  • Couples work where there is ongoing abuse. I can see one partner individually for trauma work instead.

If I'm not the right fit, I'm glad to point the person to a colleague who is.

Where and when

  • In person on Tuesdays at River Tree, 212 Heritage Park Drive, Murfreesboro.
  • By video on Wednesdays for adults anywhere in Tennessee.
  • EMDR and intensives are in person only.

Fees

Private pay. I'm not in-network with insurance plans yet. I can give a superbill for out-of-network benefits. All fees are posted on the Fees page, and everyone gets a Good Faith Estimate.

  • Want to talk first? Call me or email jamie@stonesrivertherapy.com. Please leave health details out of email and texts. We can go over them on the phone.
  • I return calls and messages within 1 business day.
  • Because of privacy rules, I can only share whether someone reached out or started with me when they give written permission.
